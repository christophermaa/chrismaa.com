I characterised the pilocarpine model of temporal lobe epilepsy with functional ultrasound imaging (fUS), extracting hemodynamic features from power-Doppler data for machine-learning classification. The work was alongside telemetric EEG electrophysiology. The thesis combined imaging physics with computational classification.

Published in the University of Helsinki Open Repository: [hdl.handle.net/10138/585176](https://hdl.handle.net/10138/585176).
