## What it is
Reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (RIS) are metasurfaces whose electromagnetic response can be switched in software. In collaboration with Aalto University's Department of Electronics and Nanoengineering, the real-time BCI pipeline drives these surfaces in a closed loop.

## Why it's interesting
The project joins two noisy physical systems, brain signals and RF hardware, through one ML decision layer. This forces the engineering to be honest about latency, failure modes and what happens when the classifier is wrong.
